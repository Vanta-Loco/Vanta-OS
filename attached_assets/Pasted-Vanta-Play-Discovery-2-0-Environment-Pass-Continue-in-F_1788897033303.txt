Continue in Free Mode. Do not switch to Max/Power.

Goal
Make Vanta Play feel like the real-world sports/activity hub of Vanta rather than a plain game list.
A user should immediately understand:
what games are happening
what sport
where
when
how many people are involved
whether they already joined
how to host or join

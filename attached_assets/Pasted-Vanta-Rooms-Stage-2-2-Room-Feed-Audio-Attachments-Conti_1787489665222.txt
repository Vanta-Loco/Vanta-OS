verify the Room exists
verify the Member is actually joined to the Room
accept one audio file
optionally accept accompanying post text
save the audio using the existing uploads directory/naming conventions
create the Room post and attachment together
Non-members must not be able to upload audio to a Room.

.mp3
.wav
.m4a
